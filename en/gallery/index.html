    Gallery

    The ready-made mapx map styles, each built from official data and added to MapLibre with one line. Pick a style to see it as a live map and copy its style URL.

    Get started for free Docs: maps

      The base map with roads, places and buildings, in four times of day.

      Calm, almost colourless base map for your own data, light or on dark charcoal.

      BEV orthophoto, optionally with streets, or a current satellite image.

      Contours, hillshading, peaks and paths for maps in the mountains, in summer or in snow colours.

      Pure relief map from the terrain model: elevation tints, with contours, or hillshade only in light and dark.

      The base map with traffic flow and traffic reports on motorways and expressways, for day and night.

    Previews and live maps use the same styles as the API. Data: BEV, GIP.at, Environment Agency Austria, ASFINAG / EVIS.AT, Copernicus Sentinel-2, mostly CC BY 4.0; the full attribution is shown on each map. Your own style from the vector tiles: Style the base map.
